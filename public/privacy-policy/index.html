What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form on this site we collect the name, firm or shop name, city, phone number, email address, product interest, indicated monthly hardware requirement and message that you enter. We do not collect payment information, and this site does not sell anything online.

How we use it

Enquiry details are used solely to respond to your enquiry, to send the catalogue or pricing you have asked for, and to contact you about supply and dealership. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Where it is stored

Submissions are delivered by email to sales@recorehardware.in and held by our form processing provider so our team can retrieve them. Google reCAPTCHA is used to block automated spam submissions; its use is subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Analytics and advertising

This site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the site and to measure advertising. These set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to any part of this site.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your enquiry details deleted, email sales@recorehardware.in and we will action the request.
