About REcore

Prepare, before you meet your architect

REcore Hardware has been supplying premium interior and furniture hardware since 2008. We work with architects, interior designers, carpenters and SME clients who need one dependable source for everything that opens, closes, slides and locks.

Every line in our catalogue is chosen for the same three things: a mechanism that still feels right after years of daily use, a finish that holds, and pricing that works on real projects — from a single flat to a full site.

  • 20 complete product lines in one price list
  • Soft-close mechanisms across drawers, slides and hinges
